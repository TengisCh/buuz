Buuz

Type Mongolian with a Latin keyboard.

Everything runs on your device. What you type is never sent anywhere.

How to type
w ү
q ө
y ы
sh ш
ch ч
yo ё
yu ю
ya я
ye е
' ь
" ъ
ii ий

o and u pick о/ө and у/ү by vowel harmony, so geree gives гэрээ and gar gives гар. To force a letter, use q or w for ө and ү.
